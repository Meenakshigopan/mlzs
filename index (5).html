FAQs

Questions parents ask


Which curriculum does the school follow?

The school follows the CBSE curriculum from Pre-school to Class XI.

What are the school timings?

Play Group to S.K.G runs from 8:00 to 11:30 AM in summer and 8:15 to 11:30 AM in winter. Classes 1 to 8 run from 8:00 AM to 2:30 PM in summer and 8:15 AM to 2:30 PM in winter.

Is transport included in the fees?

No. Transportation is not part of the shared fee structure. Please ask the office for transport details.

Is day care available?

Yes, day care and after-school activities are available.

Which sports can my child play?

Cricket, football, basketball and handball outdoors, and badminton, table tennis, carrom and chess indoors.

How can I attend parent-teacher meetings?

Meetings are held monthly and online, so parents can join from anywhere.
